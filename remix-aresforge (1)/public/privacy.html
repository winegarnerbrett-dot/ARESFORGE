Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2025 · Applies to ARESFORGE on iOS, Android, and Web

1. Data We Collect & How It Is Used

ARESFORGE minimizes personal data collection. Core workout tracking, nutrition logging, and hydration tracking work locally on your device without creating an account.

  • Training & Nutrition Records: Custom programs, completed sets, workout sessions, food logs, hydration totals, and fitness targets are stored locally on your device and synced to your private Firebase Firestore workspace only if you sign in.
  • Optional Cloud Sign-In: If you sign in with Google, ARESFORGE uses your authentication identifier and email strictly to isolate and synchronize your fitness records across devices.
  • Wearables & Health Exports: Apple Health XML/CSV/Shortcut files, Garmin files, optional Fitbit/Strava telemetry, and Bluetooth heart rate readings are processed solely to display your personal progress metrics.

2. Camera & Barcode Scanner Usage

Camera access is requested strictly when you open the food barcode scanner. Barcode decoding occurs on-device in real time. No photos or video frames are stored or uploaded. Only scanned barcode numbers are queried against Open Food Facts to return nutrition facts.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Sharing

ARESFORGE uses zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero cross-app tracking, and never sells or shares your health, workout, or nutrition data.

4. Data Export & Permanent Account Deletion

Inside the app under Profile → Privacy, Data & Support, you may export all data as JSON, erase local device data, or permanently delete your account and all cloud-stored profile, program, workout, nutrition, and metric records.
