ARESFORGE Support

Help & Troubleshooting for iOS, Android, and Web

Barcode Scanner Camera Access

Allow Camera access in iOS Settings when scanning packaged foods, or enter the UPC/EAN barcode digits manually inside the Scan Food dialog.

Apple Watch & Wearable Sync

Open Profile → Wearables inside ARESFORGE to import Apple Health XML/CSV files, paste Apple Shortcut health JSON exports, connect a Bluetooth Low Energy heart rate sensor, or connect Fitbit/Strava.

Exporting or Deleting Your Account & Data

Inside ARESFORGE, open Profile → Privacy, Data & Support to export a full JSON copy of your fitness records, erase local device storage, or permanently delete your account and all cloud records.
